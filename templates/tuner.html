Guitar Tuning Tips

  • Always tune up to the note: If a string is sharp (+cents), tune it slightly below pitch and tune up to tension to ensure tuning stability.
  • Mute other strings: Pluck one string firmly with your thumb or pick, and use your other fingers to silence neighboring strings for a pure fundamental frequency.
  • Click string buttons above: Click any string button (E2, A2, D3, G3, B3, E4) to hear a pure reference tone for tuning by ear.
